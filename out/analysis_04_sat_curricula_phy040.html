Analysis 4: PHY 040 performance by curriculum

Selected American, SABIS, and Al-Thanawiyya Al-Aama curricula; SAT Math below 600; MATH042 and PHY040 assignment recorded.

Finding: Within the verified SAT Math <600 cohort with both course assignments recorded, American Curriculum students have a mean PHY 040 Module 1 score of 25.56/50 (n=8, median 25.0, range 16.0-32.5). SABIS has only 1 recorded score, so its mean is not a reliable comparison. Al-Thanawiyya Al-Aama cannot be assessed against SAT<600 because its SAT Math values are missing.
Coverage caveat: The primary cohort is n=16; only 9 have exam scores. There are 19 missing SAT Math records across the three curricula, including every Al-Thanawiyya record.

Eligibility and assignment evidence

The SAT threshold is applied only to numeric values. A missing or dash value is not treated as a score below 600. The primary cohort also requires both course assignment flags, to verify the stated policy in the workbook.

Observations and recommendations

  • American Curriculum: mean 25.56/50, median 25.0, range 16.0-32.5, based on n=8 recorded scores.
  • SABIS: only n=1 module score is recorded, too few to characterize the curriculum.
  • Al-Thanawiyya Al-Aama: 9 records have no numeric SAT Math score. Although 5 PHY 040 scores exist in the broader curriculum group, their SAT<600 eligibility cannot be established.
  • Recommendation: do not adjust the SAT 600 rule or rank the curricula based on this limited evidence. Maintain the current route provisionally, collect consistent test, assignment, enrollment, and exam outcomes, and resolve missing SAT Math records for Al-Thanawiyya.

Limitations

  • 16 students meet the primary cohort rule, but only 9 have Module 1 results.
  • Groups are very small and unbalanced; no inferential ranking is warranted.
  • No Module 1 pass standard is specified, so results are descriptive and no pass/fail status is inferred.
