Analysis 3: Performance by curriculum

PHY 040 Module 1 Exam among students assigned to PHY 040 after a recorded Physics Placement Test failure.

Finding: Among curricula with at least 3 recorded Module 1 scores, CBSE has the highest observed mean: 31.45/50 (n=20, median 32.5). A Kruskal-Wallis test across the 5 curriculum groups with at least 3 Module 1 scores gives H=6.37, p=0.173. This does not provide statistically significant evidence of differences among these curricula at the conventional 0.05 level. It is an exploratory comparison with small and unequal groups.
Important cohort caveat: The assumed PT pass mark is 50. The cohort has 52 assigned students, but only 45 Module 1 scores. The `Enrolled in PHYS040` values are Yes=10, No=42, missing=0; they differ from the assignment field. The PT failure cutoff is an assumption; the workbook does not define the official pass score.

Method

The primary cohort comprises rows with `Take PHYS040` equal to `PHYS040` (including the workbook's `PHY040` variant) and a numeric Physics placement score below 50. The 50-point placement pass mark is an assumption because the workbook does not specify the official pass threshold. This identifies 52 assigned students. Of these, 45 have a `Phy040 Module 1 Exam (out of 50)` score and 7 have no recorded module score. Only scored records contribute to curriculum means and medians. Curriculum labels are normalized for case and whitespace (for example `A level` and `A Level` are combined); otherwise labels remain separate because the workbook provides no validated curriculum taxonomy.

The enrollment status distribution in the assigned cohort is Yes=10, No=42, missing=0. It is reported but is not used to remove course-assigned students from the cohort.

Sensitivity: explicitly enrolled students only

Restricting to records marked Enrolled in PHYS040 = Yes leaves 10 students, all with a Module 1 score. With actual enrollment restricted to Yes, CBSE has the highest mean among curricula with at least 3 scores (31.38/50, n=4). One IB Diploma student scored 35.5/50, but a one-student curriculum result cannot establish a curriculum-level effect.

Interpretation and limitations

  • Means and medians describe observed scores only; no course pass threshold is documented.
  • Groups are small and unbalanced, and 7 of 52 assigned students have no module score.
  • Curricula with fewer than 3 scored students are shown but not used as the leading curriculum-level finding.
  • The inferential test is exploratory and does not show a significant difference across the included groups at p<0.05.
  • The curriculum label is the workbook's Qual / Award field, normalized only for case and whitespace.
