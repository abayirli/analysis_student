Analysis 2: CBSE and Pakistani BISE students

Source: Engineering_Student_Profile_with_Exam_Results.xlsx | 109 exact-CBSE records | Candidate cutoffs: 75%, 70%, 60%

Recommendation: Of the three candidates, use 75% as a provisional minimum eligibility threshold for a monitored exemption pilot, not as a validated cutoff. Available data cannot establish that 70% or 75% is safe because placement outcomes among higher-mark students are nearly absent. Do not use 60%: the recorded 60%+ tested subsets show weak pass rates, particularly in Mathematics. This conclusion applies to the CBSE cohort only; Pakistani BISE students are not identifiable.
Population caveat: The workbook has 332 records and 109 with exact qualification label CBSE. It has no BISE qualification label. The 17 Pakistani-nationality students have qualification labels: A level (9), A Level (6), General Education Diploma (1), American Curriculum (1). Nationality is not used as a proxy for school board.

Method

The workbook has no explicit overall school percentage. Math Mark and Phys Mark are analyzed as subject-specific percentages; the subjects are handled independently. Placement pass is provisionally PT score ≥ 50; no official pass mark is documented. Rates and confidence intervals use only students with recorded placement scores. Later course results are reported descriptively because no course pass standard is provided.

Assessment of the three thresholds

  • 75%: most conservative candidate; use only as a temporary, monitored pilot. Not validated by current outcome data.
  • 70%: not supported over 75%; the 70-74% band has too little placement evidence.
  • 60%: not recommended. At/above 60%, Math pass rate among tested is 36% (n=11); Physics is 58% (n=26).

Operational recommendation: If choosing now, set a temporary 75% minimum in each relevant subject, subject to faculty approval and monitoring. Keep Math and Physics decisions independent. Retain testing until outcomes are captured consistently across the 60-80% range, including students otherwise eligible for exemption.

Limitations

  • No BISE qualification is identified; nationality is not a reliable proxy for school board.
  • Missingness and non-random test/course observation create selection bias.
  • The official placement pass mark and course pass criteria are not provided; 50 is a stated assumption for placement tests.
