Analysis 1: A-Level students - placement test exemption cutoff

Source: Engineering_Student_Profile_with_Exam_Results.xlsx (sheet "Engineering Enrollment"). Students with qualification "A level"/"A Level": 74. AS Level and combined qualifications (e.g. "A Level & Bachelors") are excluded.

Recommendation

  • Math: exempt students with A-Level grade B or above; students below this must enrol directly in MATH 042.
  • Physics: exempt students with A-Level grade B or above; students below this must enrol directly in PHY 040.
  • Grade C (both subjects): do not exempt - require the placement tests. Observed pass rate was 67% (n=6) in Math and 50% (n=4) in Physics, below the 75% target.
  • Grade D (both subjects): do not exempt - require the placement tests. Observed pass rate was 67% (n=3) in Math and 83% (n=6) in Physics; where a D student scores poorly the direct route (MATH 042 / PHY 040) is appropriate.
  • Mixed results (C Math / D Phys, or D Math / C Phys): do not exempt either subject; sit both placement tests and enrol in MATH 042 and/or PHY 040 based on each subject's result. Only 4 such students exist and few have PT scores, so this is a conservative judgement rather than a statistically supported cutoff.

Rule used: start at A and step down; a grade is exempt if the students holding it passed the placement test at a rate of at least 75% (PT ≥ 50); stop at the first grade that fails this test.

Read this first - data limitations and assumptions

  • Small sample: only 13 students have both an A-Level Math grade and a Math PT score, and 15 for Physics. The cutoff is indicative; confidence intervals are wide.
  • Pass mark is assumed to be 50 for both tests - the dataset does not state it. See the sensitivity table: the answer changes at a higher pass mark.
  • Many A-Level students have no letter grade (recorded as "-" or missing) or no placement score; they are excluded from the relevant calculation.
  • Students already exempted or who did not sit the test cannot be evaluated, so there may be selection bias.
  • Entries such as "50CT" are read as 50. Numeric Physics grade entries (e.g. 35) and non-letter grades are discarded.

Subject Students (n) Spearman rho (grade vs PT) p-value Median PT, grade C Median PT, grade D Mann-Whitney C vs D p-value
Math 13 0.6 0.0 50.0 50.0 0.9

A positive Spearman correlation means higher grades tend to score higher in the placement test. Grade C and D students are not distinguishable from each other on PT score (Mann-Whitney p-values are large), which is why C and D are treated the same way.
