# Analysis 3: Performance by curriculum in PHY 040

**Finding:** Among curricula with at least 3 recorded Module 1 scores, CBSE has the highest observed mean: 31.45/50 (n=20, median 32.5). This should be treated as a descriptive signal, not a statistically established ranking.

## Scope and cohort
The primary cohort comprises rows with `Take PHYS040` equal to `PHYS040` (including the workbook's `PHY040` variant) and a numeric Physics placement score below 50. The 50-point placement pass mark is an assumption because the workbook does not specify the official pass threshold. This identifies 52 assigned students. Of these, 45 have a `Phy040 Module 1 Exam (out of 50)` score and 7 have no recorded module score. Only scored records contribute to curriculum means and medians. Curriculum labels are normalized for case and whitespace (for example `A level` and `A Level` are combined); otherwise labels remain separate because the workbook provides no validated curriculum taxonomy.

The source workbook contains 332 student records. The `Take PHYS040` assignment field is used to identify students required/recommended to take the course; the separate `Enrolled in PHYS040` field is not used to exclude assigned students. In the selected cohort that field records 10 `Yes`, 42 `No`, and 0 missing. This mismatch is retained and disclosed rather than silently filtering the sample.

The leading result among curricula with n >= 3 is selected to avoid calling a one- or two-student curriculum the strongest. A Kruskal-Wallis test across the 5 curriculum groups with at least 3 Module 1 scores gives H=6.37, p=0.173. This does not provide statistically significant evidence of differences among these curricula at the conventional 0.05 level. It is an exploratory comparison with small and unequal groups.

## Interpretation
- The outcome is the PHY 040 Module 1 Exam score, reported on its recorded 0-50 scale. Higher mean and median indicate stronger observed scores.
- Small group sizes, unequal curriculum representation, missing module marks, and differences in student selection limit direct comparisons.
- A one-student group may have the numerically highest mean, but it cannot support a curriculum-level conclusion; all available curricula are included in the table and plot with their sample sizes.
- The workbook does not provide a PHY 040 module pass threshold. No pass/fail rates are inferred.
- `Take PHYS040` is treated as the assignment/required-course indicator. `Enrolled in PHYS040` differs for many cohort rows; enrollment status is reported, not used to redefine the assigned cohort.
- The PT failure threshold of 50 is an assumption; results should be rerun if the institution confirms a different pass mark.
- Confidence intervals are t-intervals for group means, intersected with the possible 0-50 exam score range; one-record groups have no interval.
