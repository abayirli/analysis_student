# Analysis 1: A-Level placement-test exemption

Under the assumed PT pass mark of 50 and the 75% step-down rule, use **B or above as a provisional exemption threshold in each subject**. Students below B should be placed directly into the corresponding foundation course: MATH 042 for Mathematics and PHY 040 for Physics. The recommendation is provisional: Mathematics has no tested grade-B students, so its B boundary is not directly validated.

## Population and method

Source: `Engineering_Student_Profile_with_Exam_Results.xlsx`, sheet `Engineering Enrollment`. The analysis includes 74 records whose `Qual / Award` is exactly `A level` or `A Level` (case-insensitive). AS Level and combined qualifications are excluded.

There are 13 students with both a recognized A-Level Mathematics letter grade and a recorded Math Placement Test score, and 15 with both a Physics grade and Physics Placement Test score. Grades are taken from `Grade Math` and `Grade Phys`; scores are from `PT MATH` and `PT PHYS`. The parser accepts numeric values and leading numbers in values such as `50CT`.

The workbook does not state the official Placement Test pass mark. This analysis assumes a score of **50 or higher is a pass**. It also uses a **75% grade-band pass-rate target**, a policy assumption rather than a value in the data. Starting at B, the rule steps down one grade at a time and stops at the first grade whose own observed pass rate is below target. Missing grades and missing PT scores are excluded from subject calculations; students previously exempted or not tested may differ systematically from the observed sample.

## Recommendation

Under the assumed PT pass mark of 50 and the 75% step-down rule, use **B or above as a provisional exemption threshold in each subject**. Students below B should be placed directly into the corresponding foundation course: MATH 042 for Mathematics and PHY 040 for Physics. The recommendation is provisional: Mathematics has no tested grade-B students, so its B boundary is not directly validated.

The grade-level evidence is consistent with stopping before C in both subjects: Mathematics C has a 67% (n=6) pass rate and Physics C has 50% (n=4), each below the 75% target. However, the Mathematics data contains no grade-B PT observations; the three students at or above B are one A and two A* students. Therefore, B is an operational cutoff suggested by the step-down rule, not a directly measured Math-B threshold. Physics has two tested B students, both passing, but this sample is also small.

## Candidate cutoff tradeoffs

For each cutoff, “Exempted who would fail PT” counts tested students who would have been exempted but scored below 50. “Required who would pass PT” counts tested students placed below the cutoff who scored at least 50. The confidence interval is a 95% Wilson interval for the pass rate among the tested exempt group.

Spearman correlation is positive in both subjects, but the samples are small. The exploratory C-versus-D Mann-Whitney tests do not show statistically significant score differences. These statistics do not establish that a grade threshold causes better placement-test performance.

## Limitations and reproducibility

- The PT pass mark (50), the target pass rate (75%), and the step-down rule are assumptions, not stated institutional standards.
- Only 13 Math and 15 Physics grade/PT pairs are available. Math has no grade-B test observations, and confidence intervals are wide.
- PT coverage may be selective because students already exempted or not tested are absent from the grade-level score analysis.
- C/D mixed-profile groups contain only 1-11 students, with even fewer PT scores. Recommendations for these combinations are conservative.
- Scores such as `50CT` are treated as 50; nonnumeric values and missing values are not scored.
