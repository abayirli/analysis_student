# Analysis 4: PHY 040 outcomes for selected curricula with SAT Math below 600

**Finding:** Within the verified SAT Math <600 cohort with both course assignments recorded, American Curriculum students have a mean PHY 040 Module 1 score of 25.56/50 (n=8, median 25.0, range 16.0-32.5). SABIS has only 1 recorded score, so its mean is not a reliable comparison. Al-Thanawiyya Al-Aama cannot be assessed against SAT<600 because its SAT Math values are missing.

## Cohort definition and data coverage
The source is `Engineering_Student_Profile_with_Exam_Results.xlsx`, sheet `Engineering Enrollment`. Curricula are selected by the exact case-insensitive `Qual / Award` labels American Curriculum, SABIS, and Al-Thanawiyya Al-Aama. The target SAT condition is a numeric SAT Math score <600. Missing values and `-` are not interpreted as below 600.
The primary performance cohort additionally requires both `Take MATH042 = MATH042` and `Take PHYS040` equal to `PHYS040` (or the workbook variant `PHY040`). This verifies the stated course requirement in the dataset. That yields 16 students. Of these, 9 have a recorded PHY 040 Module 1 exam score; the remainder are not assigned a score in the workbook.

Across the 68 records in the three curricula, 19 SAT Math values are missing or recorded as `-`. In particular, all 9 Al-Thanawiyya Al-Aama records lack a numeric SAT Math score. 5 students in that full curriculum group have a PHY 040 Module 1 score, but the dataset cannot establish whether they meet the SAT<600 criterion. They are therefore excluded from the primary cohort, not assumed to be eligible or ineligible.
There are 18 students with known SAT Math <600 across the selected curricula. 2 of these do not have both course assignment flags recorded; they are not included in the primary cohort. This documents that the workbook's assignment fields do not perfectly mirror the stated policy.

## Observations and recommendation
- **American Curriculum:** 8 scores are available among 13 students with SAT<600 and both course assignments. Mean 25.56/50, median 25.0, range 16.0-32.5. Performance varies materially, so SAT Math below 600 does not imply uniformly low PHY 040 performance.
- **SABIS:** only 1 score is present among 3 assigned students. The observed score (21.5/50) is not enough to characterize SABIS students.
- **Al-Thanawiyya Al-Aama:** the curriculum has 9 records and 5 PHY 040 scores in its broader group, but no usable SAT Math values. Its performance cannot be attributed to the under-600 policy cohort.
- **Recommendation:** continue the SAT<600 placement/foundation route provisionally, but do not use these small, incomplete Module 1 results to change the 600 threshold or rank curricula. Capture SAT Math, both assignment decisions, actual enrollment, and Module 1 results consistently for future cohorts; review Al-Thanawiyya records with Admissions because the key eligibility field is absent.

## Limitations
- The report excludes SAT Math values that are missing or marked `-`; it does not impute them.
- The primary cohort requires explicit evidence of both course assignments. Two or more SAT<600 students can lack one or both assignment flags, so policy application should be checked against source records.
- PHY 040 exam results are missing for some students with both assignment flags, and curriculum group sizes are highly uneven.
- No PHY 040 Module 1 pass threshold is specified; no pass/fail rates are inferred.
- These are descriptive outcomes, not causal estimates of curriculum effects or the impact of the SAT threshold.
